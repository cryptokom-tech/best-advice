# Candidates — The Best Advice I Ever Got

Judged locally against the published brief, in its order:
1. A single sentence about the best advice the writer ever received.
2. The advice is worth carrying: specific, earned, true beyond the moment.
3. A real voice recalling a real moment, not a slogan or fortune cookie.
4. Tight: every word earns its place, reads in one breath.
5. Lands: leaves the reader with something to keep.

## Chosen

> A nurse told me on the worst night of my life, 'You don't have to be brave — just be here in the morning.'

106 characters. It names who gave it and when, the advice survives outside that
night, and the last five words are the part a reader keeps. The em dash gives
it the pause a person would actually make saying it.
