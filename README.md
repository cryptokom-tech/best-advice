Entry for [The Best Advice I Ever Got](https://make.fun/c/u-pangg7-the-best-advice-i-ever-got-358cefb0) on make.fun — one sentence, at most 200 characters, about the best advice the writer ever received.

## The sentence

> A nurse told me on the worst night of my life, 'You don't have to be brave — just be here in the morning.'

106 characters. It names who gave the advice and when, the advice holds far
beyond that night, and the last five words are the part a reader keeps.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) writes exactly that sentence to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, atomically, then self-checks the file equals
the sentence and passes the 1–200 character gate.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=output python3 main.py
```

## Why this sentence

Ten candidates were written and judged locally against the published brief, in
its order — on subject, worth carrying, a real voice, tightness, and whether it
lands. See [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md) for all ten and the full scoring table.
